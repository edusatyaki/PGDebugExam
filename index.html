How the round works
  • Every question shows a PostgreSQL snippet with a bug. Pick the cause, the fix, or what the server actually does.
  • Some show the ERROR PostgreSQL printed when the query ran — choose the change that fixes it.
  • The test runs in fullscreen and the clock starts the moment a question appears. No pausing, no going back.
  • Leaving fullscreen, switching tabs, minimising or resizing the window, opening Inspect, or answering with a script is a violation. Each one shows a warning and is reported. The clock keeps running.
  • 10 violations and the attempt is recorded as malpractice and an alarm sounds.
  • Answer with a click or tap — or press 1–4 / A–D.
  • A correct answer scores 10, plus up to 5 more for speed. Running out of time scores 0.
  • Your progress is recorded as you go, and your result is written to the class sheet when the round ends.
  • Recorded for this round: name, section, enrolment number, network address, progress and score.
